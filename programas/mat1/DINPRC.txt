%%HP: T(3)A(R)F(.);
\<<
  @ Internal: F variable -> primitive, or a real error for unresolved INT.
  @ Caller supplies IFERR and depth guard. No global variables are changed.
  \-> F V
  \<<
    F V RISCH SIMPLIFY
    IF DUP \->STR "INT(" POS 0 >
    THEN DROP "NO PRIMITIVA ELEMENTAL" DOERR END
  \>>
\>>
